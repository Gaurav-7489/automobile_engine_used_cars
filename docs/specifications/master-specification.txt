Non-negotiable product principles
      Do not promise automatic sales. Improve measurable conversion opportunities, response speed, visibility and
       decision quality.
      Do not invent attribution, CAC or ROAS when downstream outcome data is unavailable.
      Do not force customer accounts for basic browsing or enquiry.
      Do not let website design scope silently become unlimited custom software scope.
      Do not use AI where deterministic automation is safer and sufficient.
      Human handoff remains fundamental in sales and customer communication.
      Standardize the repeatable 80%; custom-build the valuable 20%.

6. Core Data & Identity Model
Tenant hierarchy
VandLabs → Organization / Dealer Group → Brand / Dealership → Location → Users / Inventory / Leads / Campaigns /
Analytics.

Vehicle Inventory Hub
The canonical VandLabs vehicle record accepts multiple sources: manual entry, CSV/Excel, dealer DMS/CRM, partner
APIs and future marketplace adapters. External channels are integrations/projections, not uncontrolled sources of
truth.
   Stable vehicle identity + tenant/dealership/location ownership
   Source + external ID + sync state
   Make, model, variant, year, fuel, transmission, mileage, ownership, condition
   Price, availability, publish state, location
   Media, features, tags and structured specifications
   Finance/exchange eligibility and conversion context
   Audit timestamps and source-of-truth conflict rules

Progressive customer identity
Anonymous Visitor → Lead → Customer → Optional Account. No forced signup for browsing, enquiry, WhatsApp, calls
or test-drive requests.

Journey graph & attribution
Capture permitted first-party sessions, sources, campaigns, UTMs, vehicle views, searches and conversion events.
When a person identifies through an enquiry, associate allowed journey context with the lead. Preserve multiple
attribution views rather than claiming false certainty.
   first_touch
   last_touch
   lead_creation_source
   campaign_assisted
   vehicle_interest_history
   offline_outcome

7. CRM, Sales & Conversation Engine
Opinionated automotive pipeline
NEW → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → APPOINTMENT → VISITED → TEST DRIVE → NEGOTIATION →
WON / LOST / NURTURE

Dealerships may configure naming, qualification fields, required information, owners, SLA targets, reminders, lost
reasons, follow-up sequences and selected automation rules - without turning the product into a generic CRM.

Lead context
     Customer identity and contact permissions
     Interested vehicle(s)
     Source and campaign
     Assigned salesperson/team
     Conversation timeline
     Tasks and follow-ups
     Appointments and test drives
     Finance and exchange intent
     Stage history and outcome

Progressive omnichannel conversation engine
WhatsApp is first-class, but the data model is channel-neutral. WhatsApp, phone, website chat, forms, email and
future channels feed one timeline. Basic dealers may use click-to-chat; Growth/Scale deployments can add approved
WhatsApp APIs, templates, routing, reminders and automation.

    HUMAN HANDOFF AI or automation may qualify, summarize and assist, but must never become a wall
    between a buyer and a salesperson.




Finance & exchange orchestration
V1 captures structured finance and exchange intent and routes it into CRM. It does not pretend to approve loans or
guarantee valuations. Later provider adapters can connect banks/NBFCs, valuation systems and inspection partners.

8. Analytics, Intelligence & AI
Automotive Intelligence Command Center
   Business: leads, qualified leads, test drives, negotiations, sales outcomes
   Acquisition: channel, campaign, source and landing-page performance
   Vehicle demand: views → enquiries → test drives → sales by vehicle/segment
   Sales funnel: stage conversion and leakage
   Operations: uncontacted leads, SLA breaches, overdue follow-ups, today's test drives
   Economics: only when reliable cost and outcome data exist
   Intelligence: evidence-backed patterns and recommended areas to investigate
Operating loop: MEASURE → DIAGNOSE → EXPERIMENT → LEARN

Hybrid vehicle discovery
Structured filters remain authoritative. Natural-language search can interpret intent such as 'automatic SUV under 15
lakh with low kilometres', but vehicle facts always come from verified inventory records. Search behavior becomes
demand intelligence over time.

AI architecture
AI-ready, not AI-dependent. Automobile Engine uses a VandLabs AI Gateway / Model Router so application code is not
hardwired to one model provider. AI may assist classification, summarization, qualification, retrieval and
recommendations; consequential actions remain controlled.
   Structured-output validation
   Prompt/version management
   Guardrails and permission checks
   Cost/latency observability
   Evaluation datasets
   Provenance for retrieval-backed answers
   No silent consequential actions

9. Multi-Tenancy, Security & Permissions
Standard dealerships use strong shared multi-tenancy; enterprise groups can move to dedicated
databases/environments when justified. Isolation is enforced at multiple layers rather than relying on a single
tenant_id filter.
     Database tenant scoping + PostgreSQL RLS where appropriate
     Service-layer authorization on every protected action
     Tenant-scoped cache, search indexes and analytics
     Tenant-scoped object-storage paths/policies
     Automated cross-tenant isolation tests
     Audit logging for privileged actions
     Controlled, temporary and auditable VandLabs support access

    Dealer Group Owner                                       Entire organization / multiple dealerships


    Dealership Admin                                         Specific dealership


    Location Manager                                         One or more locations

11. Integrations, Automation & Platform Operations
VandLabs Integration Hub
Core modules talk to normalized VandLabs interfaces rather than individual vendors. Provider adapters handle
DMS/CRM, WhatsApp BSPs, Meta/Google, finance, valuation, email/SMS and future marketplace connections.
Representative internal contracts: sendMessage(), createLead(), syncVehicle(), updateInventory(), createFinanceEnquiry(),
recordCampaignEvent(), scheduleFollowUp().


n8n may power dealership-specific workflows, rapid experiments and unusual integrations, but core business logic
must not depend on n8n.

Managed automated provisioning
New dealership onboarding provisions organization, locations, brand/design profile, domain, staff/permissions,
inventory source, contact channels, analytics, CRM defaults, entitlements, SEO configuration and QA.

Platform Control Center
    Dealerships, dealer groups, onboarding and domains
    Entitlements and feature rollout
    Integration/webhook/job health
    Usage, storage and AI cost signals
    System health, errors and deployment versions
    Support and controlled tenant access

Feature control
Feature flags control rollout/experiments; entitlements control what a dealership has purchased or is allowed to use.
These are separate systems. Commercial packages are bundles over modular capabilities, not separate codebases.

12. Engineering Delivery Standard
Repository
Professional pnpm + Turborepo monorepo. Suggested boundaries: apps for dealership web, admin/command center
and API; packages for UI, tokens, schemas, contracts, analytics, auth, SEO, media and config; infrastructure/aws;
tests/tooling.

State & forms
Server-first state. TanStack Query for interactive server data, Zustand for small global client state, React local state for
local UI, URL parameters for shareable/filter state, React Hook Form + schema validation for forms. Backend
independently validates contracts.

CI/CD
Branch → PR → lint/typecheck → unit/integration/contract tests → build + bundle budget → security scan → Playwright
→ preview → staging → production → monitoring. Use feature flags, safe migrations, rollback and canary/blue-green
patterns for higher-risk backend changes.

Testing
   Unit + component tests
   Integration + API contract tests
   Playwright critical-path E2E
   Visual regression
   Accessibility checks
   Performance/Core Web Vitals budgets
   Load testing for critical APIs
   Security and tenant-isolation tests
   Cross-browser/device QA

Launch QA
   Every CTA works and carries correct vehicle context
   Forms create correct lead records and attribution
   WhatsApp links/messages carry vehicle context
   Vehicle data, price, availability and media are verified
   Analytics events fire once with correct tenant/source/vehicle context
   Privacy/consent and opt-out behavior is correct
   SEO/canonicals/structured data/sitemaps are correct
   No test leads, placeholder credentials or debug data remain

13. Agent Build Directive
 INSTRUCTION TO EVERY IMPLEMENTATION AGENT Build the VandLabs Automobile Engine as a production-
 grade, multi-tenant automotive commerce and growth platform. Do not reduce it to a dealership template,
 generic CRM, workshop ERP or AI demo.




Agent priorities
1. Preserve the domain boundaries in this specification.
2. Keep V1 focused on the Proof-of-Engine scope; do not silently add future modules.
3. Prefer shared platform capabilities over dealership-specific forks.
4. Keep customer-facing experiences premium, restrained, fast and highly responsive.
5. Treat vehicle, lead, customer, tenant and attribution data as authoritative business data.
6. Never fabricate analytics, attribution, vehicle facts, availability or business outcomes.
7. Keep AI assistive and controlled; deterministic systems remain authoritative.
8. Enforce tenant isolation and server-side authorization regardless of UI behavior.
9. Use contract-first interfaces so frontend, backend, integrations and AI can evolve independently.
10. Optimize aggressively without sacrificing the premium visual standard.
11. Every feature must work across organization → dealership → location scopes where relevant.
12. When a requirement conflicts with this document, surface the conflict instead of guessing.

Reference implementation success path
Campaign / Google / social → dealership landing experience → inventory → vehicle detail →
WhatsApp / form / call / test-drive → lead record → qualification → follow-up → visit/test drive →
outcome → dashboard → optimization.

Definition of done for the first reference dealership
A polished, realistic used-car dealership can be provisioned on its own domain, publish verified inventory, attract and
attribute traffic, convert vehicle interest into structured leads, operate those leads through a basic sales pipeline, and
view trustworthy operational and conversion reporting - all on the shared VandLabs platform without custom-forking
the core.

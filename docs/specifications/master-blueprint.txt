01 / Mandate, baseline & decision rules
CURRENT BLUEPRINT + PROPOSED

The commercial shift is to sell a dealership growth system, not a website. The website is the front door of an
operating ecosystem that joins inventory, discovery, vehicle evaluation, enquiry, WhatsApp/call/form,
qualification, appointment/test drive, sale, reporting and optimization. Existing channels and people remain
valuable participants.

The original working name, VandLabs Dealership Digital Growth Ecosystem, remains the baseline service
architecture. VANDLABS USED-CAR AI ECOSYSTEM is its expanded product identity. The intelligence layer
does not remove Google Business, campaign destinations, follow-up templates, reporting or the
existing-agency model.


 Decision                      Baseline / expansion


 Architecture                  Standardize the repeatable 80%; custom-build the commercially justified 20%. These are
                               source principles, not measured ratios.


 Outcome promise               Improve journey quality, measurement, response discipline and opportunities. Do not
                               guarantee sales, revenue, profit or resale value.


 Data truth                    Dealership-approved structured records govern price, availability, specifications and
                               policy. LLM text cannot override them.


 Execution gate                Ship a usable pilot before predictive models, broad market coverage or self-service SaaS.
                               Tenant isolation is designed from day one.


 Commercial gate               Low-ticket packages cannot silently absorb a custom intelligence platform. Advanced
                               engineering requires a separate statement of work.




02 / Executive summary
CURRENT BLUEPRINT + PROPOSED

VandLabs connects fragmented dealership operations into a measurable operating system. Dealers retain
Marketplace, OLX, Instagram, Google, referrals, walk-ins, their CRM and their agency. VandLabs provides
the owned conversion layer, vehicle context, lead operations, automation and decision-support intelligence
around them.

The first pilot proves a complete operational loop: inventory → vehicle page → enquiry → WhatsApp → lead
record → qualification → follow-up → appointment → dashboard. Manager-confirmed outcomes feed
reporting. A click is not a conversation, a lead is not a qualified buyer, and an attributed sale is not a
guaranteed causal result.

The expansion treats inventory as capital: track cost, age, demand, margin, comparable asking prices and
holding cost. Produce reviewable actions for pricing, acquisition, aged-stock exit and lead rescue. Market
Opportunity Radar identifies potential acquisition opportunities based on permissioned market
observations; a human inspects and decides.


 Audience                                 What this document enables


 Founder / product manager                Approve product scope, pilot gates, unit economics, ownership and priorities.

03 / Product thesis
CURRENT BLUEPRINT + PROPOSED


“Your inventory is your capital.
VandLabs helps you decide where to put it, how to price
it, how to sell it, and when to get your money out.”
Secondary positioning: “VandLabs builds the digital growth infrastructure around a used-car dealership.”
The core sales explanation is: “We connect your inventory, customer enquiries, follow-up, marketing and
intelligence into one system.”

Inventory intelligence is decision support. It helps an owner investigate a vehicle or review an asking price;
it does not inspect a car, settle legal title, approve finance, guarantee depreciation or promise a buyer.
Salespeople own negotiation and relationships. Dealers retain control over purchasing, pricing and exits.

D03 / Inventory lifecycle


  New arrival                                  Active                                       Reserved
  Validate + approve                           Available + published                        Expiry + confirmation




  Delivered / archived                         Sold                                         Under negotiation
  Retention + history                          Atomic sale + delist                         Owner + next action




Reserved can expire back to Active; negotiation can fail back to Active. Test drive is a schedulable operational flag/event, not
proof that availability changed. Delivered/Archived follow sale; returns require a manager-approved reversal event.


Display all requested statuses: New arrival, Active, Reserved, Under negotiation, Test drive, Sold, Delivered,
Archived. Implementation uses stock status plus a Test drive flag, avoiding contradictory exclusive states
when several leads negotiate or a car has a booked drive. Listing status (draft/published/withdrawn) is
separate. Status transitions require permitted roles, reason, timestamp and optimistic version check.


   IN SIMPLE TERMS

   One edit changes the approved record. The system updates connected destinations and keeps a
   history of who changed what. A failed external update remains visible rather than pretending every
   channel is current.

M08 / Inventory health score - build contract

 Contract                   Specification


 WHAT / WHY                 A decision-support indicator combines demand, age, competitiveness, enquiry velocity,
                            market position, margin, depreciation risk and liquidity.


 USER                       Owner; manager


 INPUT                      Clean vehicle metrics; comparable coverage; configured weights and cohort.


 SYSTEM PROCESS             Normalize each available dimension to 0–100; weighted mean over available dimensions;
                            publish coverage separately; suppress composite when required data is absent. Explain
                            drivers and model version.


 OUTPUT                     Illustrative 82/100 with strong demand/competitive price/healthy age; 31/100 with weak
                            demand/above-market price/age/capital risk.


 DATA REQUIRED              Age, views, qualified enquiries, price snapshot, comparable cohort, known costs; observation
                            window.


 AI REQUIRED?               No LLM for score. Rules/statistics first; optional narrative.


 INTEGRATION                Internal events; permitted comparables for price component.
 REQUIRED?


 EDGE CASES                 Thin traffic, new arrival, unequal ad exposure, missing cost or comparables. Do not score
                            missing dimensions as zero.


 SECURITY                   Owner-only margin dimension; redact private economics for sales/marketing.


 SUCCESS / DEFINITION       Every score exposes contributing inputs, coverage, timestamp and version; fixture tests
 OF DONE                    stable; missing data causes partial/withheld score; manager can challenge it.


ASSUMPTION - calibration proposal: demand 20%, enquiry velocity 15%, age 15%, competitiveness
15%, margin 10%, depreciation risk 10%, liquidity 10%, market position 5%. Days in stock belongs inside
age, not an additional double-counted penalty. Demand uses exposure-adjusted engagement; liquidity is
historical time-to-sale evidence only where sufficient. Weights require pilot calibration; the score is not
scientifically exact.

56 / Final product thesis
CURRENT BLUEPRINT + PROPOSED

VandLabs connects the fragmented dealership ecosystem. It is not trying to replace Marketplace, OLX,
Google, Instagram, the existing CRM, agency or salespeople. The dealership keeps the channels that
already work; VandLabs creates the intelligence and infrastructure around them.

The website stays the front door. The value grows when inventory, acquisition, enquiry, follow-up, sales
outcomes and capital decisions share a reliable operating layer. Standardize delivery, scope custom work
honestly, protect client data, show evidence and let the right human make the decision.

The product succeeds when it is understood quickly, deployed reliably, operated every month and
connected to measurable decisions. First prove the core loop; then add inventory and market intelligence
where permissioned data and validated methods support it.



“Your inventory is your capital.
VandLabs helps you decide where to put it, how to price
it, how to sell it, and when to get your money out.”
VandLabs builds the digital growth infrastructure around a used-car dealership.
